Editorial policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This page explains how The Daily Globe produces its original journalism and how we handle the headlines we link to from other publishers.

1. Who is responsible

The Daily Globe is owned and operated by Omar Abdirahman Mohamed, trading as The Daily Globe. Our editor, Sayid Omar, is responsible for everything we publish under our own name, including our articles and Daily Briefings. Each article carries the author's name and the date it was published, and the date it was updated if we have changed it since.

2. Original articles

Our articles explain important stories and policies in plain English. We write every article ourselves. We do not copy or lightly rewrite other outlets' stories, and we do not publish text produced automatically without human research, writing and checking.

  • Sources first. Facts and figures come from primary or authoritative sources wherever possible: official statistics (for example the Office for National Statistics), regulators and public bodies (the Bank of England, Ofgem, the Office for Budget Responsibility, GOV.UK), international organisations (the UN, NATO, the WHO, the IEA) and established news organisations for events.
  • Every article lists its sources. We link 2 or more sources at the end of each piece so readers can check what we say.
  • No invented quotes or numbers. We only quote people when the quote appears in a published source we link to, and we never make up statistics. Where a figure is an estimate or forecast, we say so.
  • Clear separation of fact and analysis. Explainers set out how things work and what has been announced. Where we add analysis or judgement, we make clear that it is our view.
  • No advice. Articles about money, health or law are general information, not personal financial, medical or legal advice.

3. Checking before publication

Before an article is published, the editor checks each figure, date and name against the linked source, checks that links work, and reads the piece for fairness and clarity.

4. Corrections and updates

If we get something wrong, we will correct it promptly. Significant corrections are noted at the end of the article with the date of the change. When an article is updated with new information, the "Updated" date at the top changes. To report an error, email cumar9911@gmail.com with the link and a short description.

5. Independence, advertising and affiliate links

The Daily Globe is funded by advertising. Advertisers have no influence over what we write, and ads are always visually separate from our journalism. A small number of guides may contain affiliate links; where they do, we label them clearly and mark the links as sponsored. We do not accept payment to publish articles.

6. The headline feed

Our section pages show headlines, short summaries and images from the public RSS feeds of the publishers named on each item, under their licence terms (see About us). Every item is credited to its publisher and links to the original article. We do not republish full articles from other publishers, and we do not present their work as ours. Because these pages contain no original writing, we ask search engines not to index them.

7. Use of tools

We may use software tools to help with research, spelling and formatting. The editor is responsible for the facts, wording and judgement in every article, and checks every fact against the sources listed.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, corrections or complaints: cumar9911@gmail.com.
